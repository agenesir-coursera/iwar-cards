How iWAR is computed

iWAR = [(FP+ − 100) × wFP
    + (RE+ − 100) × wRE
    + (CP+ − 100) × wCP
    − (CR+ − 100) × wCR] × Leverage
Effort and Audience are shown alongside iWAR but not mixed into the score — use them for triage decisions (rebuild cost vs. reach).
Replacement-level item iWAR = 0. Negative means below replacement. Star tiers: ≥ +5 MVP, ≥ +3 All-Star, ≥ +1 Starter.
